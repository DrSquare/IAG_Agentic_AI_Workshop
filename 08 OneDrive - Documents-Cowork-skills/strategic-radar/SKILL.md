# Strategic Radar

## Purpose
Give the Group Executive a five-minute read each Monday: the five developments from the last 7 days most likely to affect our FY27 priorities, each with a "so what" and a suggested owner.

## Priorities (from FY27 Strategy on a Page, SharePoint > Strategy; review each quarter)
1. Motor & home margins (owner: Priya Nair)
2. Natural-hazard pricing & reinsurance (owner: Priya Nair)
3. Claims cost & experience (owner: Daniel Okafor)
4. Digital & AI productivity (owner: Sam Taylor)
5. Regulatory standing (owner: Olivia Grant)

If the strategy document has changed, use the document, not this list.

## Sources: scan five lenses separately
1. Market & customer: affordability, premium trends, natural-hazard events, broker and distribution moves.
2. Competitors: ASX announcements, results, launches and leadership changes of listed and major Australian general insurers and insurtechs.
3. Technology: AI in claims, pricing and underwriting; core platform and vendor news.
4. Regulatory: apra.gov.au, asic.gov.au, treasury.gov.au, afca.org.au, insurancecouncil.com.au (consultations, speeches, enforcement, standards).
5. Internal: my email, Teams channels #strategy and #pricing, and Board papers from the last 7 days.

## Scoring
- Impact on each priority: High / Medium / Low.
- Urgency: Now (this week) / This quarter / Watch.
- Top 5 = highest impact first, then urgency. The next 5 go on the watchlist.

## Output format
Email to me, subject "Strategic Radar - w/c [Monday's date]":
1. Headline: three lines.
2. Top 5, each as: What happened | So what for Harbourline | Suggested owner | Source (link and date).
3. Watchlist: five one-liners.
4. Three questions for the ELT.
Also save a Word copy to Documents/Cowork/Radar/.

## Rules
- Only items dated within the last 7 days. Leave out anything older, even if relevant.
- Link every claim to its source. Never state a competitor fact without a link.
- If a lens has nothing material, write "No material change" rather than padding.
- Draft only. Send to no one but me unless I approve.
