# Competitor Profile

## Scope
Australian business, last 12 months, unless told otherwise.

## Headings (use exactly these, in this order)
1. Results & outlook: GWP growth, insurance trading ratio or COR, guidance, capital position.
2. Pricing & affordability: premium rate moves by line, public statements on pricing, affordability initiatives.
3. Product & digital: launches, channels, partnerships, AI and claims initiatives.
4. Customer voice: themes from ProductReview.com.au, Google and Trustpilot reviews, and AFCA complaint data.
5. Corporate moves: M&A, reinsurance programme, leadership changes, regulatory action.

## Source rules
- Cite and date every fact with a link.
- Prefer company filings (ASX announcements, results presentations, annual reports) over media.
- Flag anything older than 12 months.
- If a source needs a sign-in or blocks access, say so and move on; don't guess.

## Output
One Markdown file per company: research/{company}.md.
Synthesis: research/summary.md with a side-by-side table, 5 key differentiators, where each company is gaining or losing ground, and 3 implications for Harbourline. Also research/competitor-matrix.xlsx with one row per company and one column per heading.

## Refresh
When rerun, compare with the previous summary and lead with what changed.
