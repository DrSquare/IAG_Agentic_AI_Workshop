# Stakeholder Brief

## Inputs
- The meeting invite and attendee list.
- Key stakeholder list: SharePoint > Stakeholders > key-stakeholders.xlsx.
- My email, Teams chats and meeting recaps involving the stakeholder's organisation in the last 90 days.
- SharePoint > Partners > [Organisation] (account plan, QBR recaps).
- The organisation's recent public news.

## Definitions
- **Commitment:** a promise with an owner and a date (explicit or clearly implied), made in email, chat or a meeting. Track both sides: what we promised and what they promised.
- **Status:** done (evidence it happened), open (due date not yet passed), overdue (due date passed, no evidence it happened).
- **Temperature:** warming / steady / cooling, judged on tone, responsiveness, open issues and competitive signals. Always give the reason.

## Output format
1-page Word document "Stakeholder Brief - [Name] - [date]":
1. Snapshot: who they are, relationship value, relationship owner.
2. Their top 3 priorities and concerns, each with evidence.
3. Commitments table: commitment | by whom | due | status | source.
4. Relationship temperature and why.
5. Handle with care: sensitive topics.
6. Three asks I should make.

Then add each open or overdue commitment as a row in Documents/Cowork/Stakeholders/commitments-tracker.xlsx (Tracker sheet): stakeholder, commitment, owner, due, status, source. Don't duplicate rows that already exist; update their status instead.

## Rules
- Cite a source for every commitment.
- Mark anything older than 60 days "check still current".
- Never contact the stakeholder. Draft follow-ups only for my approval.
